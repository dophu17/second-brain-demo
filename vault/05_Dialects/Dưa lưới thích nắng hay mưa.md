Dưa lưới thích nắng hay mưa (Cucumber likes sunshine more than rain) is a Vietnamese idiom that conveys the preference for sunny weather over rainy weather. The idiom suggests that people generally enjoy and appreciate the benefits of sunshine more than the inconvenience and discomfort caused by rain.
